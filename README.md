# My First Repository  
*A simple intro inspired by the world of Harry Potter*

---

## Intro
**Harry Potter** is a magical story that shows how friendship can shine even when the world feels dark —  
just like how H~2~O looks simple but is still incredibly powerful.

---

## Magic & Lessons  
Harry learns many spells, including:

- **Lumos** — the spell that brings light into darkness  
- *Expelliarmus* — his signature spell  
- `Wingardium Leviosa` — the classic levitation charm  

> “Happiness can be found even in the darkest of times, if one only remembers to turn on the light.”  
> — *Albus Dumbledore*

---

## Strange Discovery  
Harry faces huge battles,  
and one of them *totally proves* that **the world is flat**.  
(*This is clearly a joke — but it stays in for comedy.*)

---

## Important Theme  
His journey reminds us that  
==very important words==
